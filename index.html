About Thanjavur Thalayatti Bommai

Thanjavur Thalayatti Bommai is a traditional handmade doll from Tamil Nadu. It is famous for its unique balancing and dancing movement.

These beautiful dolls represent the rich culture, art and traditional craftsmanship of Thanjavur.
